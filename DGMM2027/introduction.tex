\section{Introduction}


Intrinsic dimension analysis of data.

Fukunaga and Olsen \cite{Fukunaga:1971} dimension analysis through the spectrum of local covariance matrix.

Levina et Bickel \cite{Levina:2004} Maximum Likelihood Estimation of
Intrinsic Dimension, local approximation with Poisson process.

Farahmand et al. \cite{Farahmand:2007} dimension analysis by measuring the logarithm of the growth of the number of points within a ball, with theoretic guarantees

Allard et al. \cite{Allard:2012} multiscale decomposition by tree decomposition, affine approximation, difference by affine approximation (similarly to wavelets).

Common drawback: a curved curve in $\R^d$ has locally an affine
dimension that may be greater than 1, hence affine approximation may
fail.
